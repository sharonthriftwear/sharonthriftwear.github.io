Delivery & payments

Pickup or delivery across Kenya

Pick up in Kiambu Town, opposite Rumathi Building. You can also arrange rider delivery within Kiambu or Nairobi, or request courier delivery to another town. Sharon will confirm the fee and timing when you order.

Pay by M-Pesa after confirmation

Confirm availability and the total on WhatsApp before paying. In M-Pesa, choose Lipa na M-Pesa → Buy Goods and Services and use the shop’s till.
